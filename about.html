About Us

From Dental Claim Desk to Lumora Healthcare Services

We started as a small dental billing desk. Today, as Lumora Healthcare Services, the trade name of Lumora Healthcare LLP, we manage the whole revenue cycle for three dental offices, and we use what we learn there for every client.

Why Lumora

Lumora means light. Billing should be clear: every office should know what was billed, what insurance paid, and what the patient owes.

Our name changed because our scope grew. Our commitment to accuracy and plain communication has not changed.

Milestones

  • Started as Dental Claim Desk Foundation
  • Took on full RCM for our first offices Growth
  • Registered as Lumora Healthcare LLP, trading as Lumora Healthcare Services Today
  • Three offices, full-cycle billing Live
How we work

Because we run billing daily, we notice the small things

We stay in the day-to-day

We work inside live offices, with live schedules and live insurance portals. Our advice comes from running the work, not from a slide deck.

We explain the why

Every denial, adjustment and write-off comes with a plain-language explanation your staff can learn from.

We protect patient data

Billing involves protected health information. We work on HIPAA-aligned practices: limited access, secure systems, documented handling.
